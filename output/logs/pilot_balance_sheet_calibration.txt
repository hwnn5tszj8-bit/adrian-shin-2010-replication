4848: Balance Sheet and Financial Leverage
4849: ------------------------------------
4850: 
4851: Asset Composition
4852: 
4853: The Company's actual level of balance sheet capital, capital requirements and
4854: thereby the level of financial leverage, is a function of numerous variables,
4855: including asset composition, rating agency/creditor perception, business
4856: prospects, regulatory requirements, balance sheet liquidity, cost/availability
4857: of capital and risk of loss. The Company consistently maintains a highly liquid
4858: balance sheet, with the vast majority of the Company's assets consisting of
4859: cash, marketable securities inventories and collateralized receivables arising
4860: from customer-related and proprietary securities transactions.
4861: 
4862: Collateralized receivables consist of resale agreements secured predominantly by
4863: U.S. government and agency securities, customer margin loans and securities
4864: borrowed, which are typically secured by marketable corporate debt and equity
4865: securities. The nature of the Company's business as a securities dealer requires
4866: it to carry significant levels of securities inventories to meet its customer
4867: and proprietary trading needs. Additionally, the Company's role as a financial
4868: intermediary for customer activities, which it conducts on a principal basis,
4869: together with its customer-related activities in its clearance business, results
4870: in significant levels of customer-related balances, including customer margin
4871: debt, securities borrowed and reverse repurchase activity. The Company's total
4872: assets and financial leverage can and do fluctuate, depending largely on
4873: economic and market conditions, volume of activity and customer demand.
4874: 
4875: The Company's total assets at February 29, 2008 increased to $399.0 billion from
4876: $395.4 billion at November 30, 2007. The increase was primarily attributable to
4877: increases in cash and securities deposited with clearing organizations or
4878: segregated in compliance with federal regulations, securities borrowed, and
4879: financial instruments owned, at fair value, partially offset by a decrease in
4880: assets of variable interest entities and mortgage loan special purpose entities.
4881: 
4882: Balance Sheet Size and Variability
4883: 
4884: Given the nature of the Company's market-making and customer-financing activity,
4885: the overall size of the balance sheet fluctuates from time to time. The
4886: Company's total assets at each quarter end are typically lower than would be
4887: observed on an average basis. At the end of each quarter, the Company typically
4888: uses excess cash to finance high-quality, highly liquid securities inventory
4889: that otherwise would be funded via the repurchase agreement market. In addition,
4890: the Company reduces its matched book repurchase and reverse repurchase
4891: activities at quarter end. Finally, the Company may reduce the aggregate level
4892: of inventories through ordinary course, open market activities in the most
4893: liquid portions of the balance sheet, which are principally U.S. government and
4894: agency securities and agency mortgage pass-through securities.
4895: 
4896:  At February 29, 2008 and November 30, 2007, total assets of $399.0 billion and
4897: $395.4 billion were approximately 12.4% and 12.2%, respectively, lower than the
4898: average of the month-end balances observed over the trailing 12-month period.
4899: Despite fluctuations in total assets at each quarter end, the Company's overall
4900: market, credit and liquidity risk profile does not change materially, since the
4901: reduction in asset balances is predominantly in highly liquid, short-term
4902: instruments that are financed on a secured basis. This periodic reduction
4903: verifies the inherently liquid nature of the balance sheet and provides
4904: consistency with respect to creditor constituents' evaluation of the Company's
4905: financial condition.
4906: 
4907: Leverage Ratios
4908: 
4909: Balance sheet leverage measures are one approach to assessing the capital
4910: adequacy of a securities firm, such as the Company. Gross leverage equals total
4911: assets divided by stockholders' equity, inclusive of preferred and trust
4912: preferred equity. The Company views its trust preferred equity as a component of
4913: its equity capital base given the equity-like characteristics of the securities.
4914: The Company also receives rating agency equity credit for these securities. Net
4915: adjusted leverage equals net adjusted assets divided by tangible equity capital,
4916: which excludes goodwill and intangible assets from both the numerator and the
4917: denominator, as equity used to support goodwill and intangible assets is not
4918: available to support
